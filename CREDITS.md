## Art and sound

Every sprite (the duck, skins, hats, glasses, neckwear, toys, icons) is **original pixel art**
made for this project, as `.pxl` text files in `assets/src/`. No asset was copied, traced or
imported from another game or app. Sounds are tiny chiptune recipes generated by code
(`src/core/sound`): there are no sound files. MIT licensed with the rest of the project.

## Services (only when you turn them on)

- **[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/)**: weather and city search, only after you type a city

- **GitHub Releases**: "Check for updates" (opt-in).
- **Cloudflare Workers**: the Friends relay (opt-in; it only carries encrypted boxes). Anyone can
